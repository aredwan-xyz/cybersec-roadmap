# Code of conduct

## The standard

- Be respectful. Criticize ideas and content, not people.
- Assume good intent, and explain your reasoning.
- Beginners are welcome here. No question is too basic.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.

## Specific to this subject

- Do not share, request or encourage testing of systems without authorization.
- Do not post real credentials, personal data or details of unreported vulnerabilities in other people's products.
- If you find a real vulnerability somewhere, report it to the owner through their disclosure process, not here.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and comments that break this standard, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
