# Limits of this roadmap

A roadmap that hides its weak points is not worth following. These are the known ones.

## Evidence

- **Market figures are second-hand.** The demand numbers in [market/focus-2026.md](../market/focus-2026.md) come from search summaries of industry surveys and news coverage, several of them from vendors. One source disagreed with another on the same statistic. Treat them as directional.
- **The recommendation is not validated against job postings.** It rests on what surveys say employers want, not on a count of real AI security or OT security roles, their seniority, or where they are open. That is why the 90-day plan starts by having you read 20 postings yourself.
- **"Builders have a head start" is plausible and unproven.** Hiring managers may still want security experience first.
- **The OT and smart-building niche has the weakest evidence:** agreement that talent is scarce, and no posting counts.

## Scope

- **Eligibility is on you to check.** Many security roles in government, defence, banking and critical infrastructure require a clearance or background screening, which can depend on citizenship, residency history or immigration status.
- **90 days does not make a penetration tester.** At the end you can run a basic security check on an LLM application you built or were authorized to review. Describe it that way.
- **The legal templates are learning drafts.** Get a lawyer's input before using [the scope and authorization template](../templates/scope-and-authorization.md) for paid work.
- **Classical adversarial machine learning is left out on purpose.** So are general penetration testing and Active Directory in the first 90 days.

## Content

- **The labs are specifications.** No lab code ships with this repo.
- **Resources were checked for loading, not for content.** A scheduled check requests every link. It cannot tell you whether a course is still good, still free, or still current.
- **Certification details change.** Names, versions, prices and prerequisites are from the author's knowledge at the time of writing. Check the vendor's page.
- **The field moves fast.** AI security guidance written this quarter can be stale next quarter. The plan asks you to review it at day 45 and day 90.

## Process

- **Checkpoints are self-graded** unless you find a reviewer. The plan asks you to find one.
- **An alternative was not weighed in depth:** staying in your current line of work and adding a security section to what you already sell, without a career change. The plan's exit criteria point back to it.

## Found another gap?

Open an issue. Corrections to this page are the most useful contributions the repo can get.
