## Out of scope

List explicitly: third-party services, other tenants, production data, social engineering, denial-of-service testing, physical access.

## Permitted activities

For example: design review, review of prompts and tool definitions, running an agreed test suite against a test environment, manual prompt-injection testing with synthetic data.

## Not permitted

For example: accessing real user data, changing or deleting data, testing outside the agreed window, sharing findings with anyone but the named contacts.

## Handling of data and findings

- Test data is synthetic.
- Any real data encountered by accident is reported immediately, not copied, and not retained.
- Findings are delivered to the named contact only, and kept confidential until the owner agrees otherwise.
- Assessor's notes and evidence are deleted or returned on an agreed date.
